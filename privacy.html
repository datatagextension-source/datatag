DataTag

Privacy policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

The short version

DataTag runs entirely inside your browser. It has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Nothing it sees or stores is ever sent to us or anyone else.

What DataTag reads

  • Email addresses and phone numbers you enter into web forms. DataTag notes them when you leave the field so it can recognise them in outgoing requests. It never reads password fields.
  • Requests that web pages send to other companies' domains (their address and contents), so it can check whether they contain your email or phone number. Requests to the site you're on are ignored.

What DataTag stores, and where

  • Detection results for each tab (which companies received your data, and in what form) and the values you typed are kept in your browser's session storage. They're cleared when you close the tab or quit the browser.
  • Identifiers you save in Settings are kept in your browser's local storage on this device. They're never synced to other devices. Remove them at any time in Settings, or by uninstalling DataTag.
  • Request contents are held briefly in memory for re-checking and are never written to disk.

What DataTag shares

Nothing. DataTag makes no network requests of its own. Share reports are generated on your device, never include your email address or phone number, and are only shared if you choose to download or copy one.

Why it needs its permissions

  • Read and change all your data on all websites / webRequest: needed to see the requests pages send to third parties, which is the whole purpose of the extension. DataTag only observes; it never changes or blocks requests or page content.
  • Storage: for the session results and saved identifiers described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with an updated date above. DataTag will never start sending data off your device without asking you first.
