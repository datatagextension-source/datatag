DataTag

See who gets your email the moment you type it.

Many signup pages share your email or phone number with ad platforms and data companies, often before you press submit. DataTag shows you who, as it happens.

How it works

  1. 1
    You fill in a form as usual

    When you leave an email or phone field, DataTag notes what you entered.

  2. 2
    It checks everything the page sends to other companies

    Including hashed, encoded and compressed data — the forms trackers use to hide it.

  3. 3
    The icon shows how many companies received it

    Click it to see who they are and exactly how your data was sent.

Your data stays with you

  • Everything runs inside your browser. DataTag has no servers and sends nothing anywhere.
  • What it detects on each page is cleared when you close Chrome.
  • Identifiers you save in Settings are stored only in this browser and never synced.
  • DataTag doesn't block anything, so sites keep working normally.

What it can't see

  • What a company's own servers do with your data after you sign up.
  • Data that's encrypted before it's sent. DataTag flags these requests as "unreadable" so you know they exist.
